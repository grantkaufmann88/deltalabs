# Mask render source

The current film uses the user's `CacheCAD/Design/Hardware/SOLIDWORKS/SleepMask.step` export for macro copper, pads, soldermask, and white silkscreen. The thin temple polyimide beneath D20–D22 comes from the mask's native PCB geometry because that region is absent from the STEP substrate mesh; the macro still shows only one substrate. The folded tab shots use one SolidWorks PCB mesh at a time, with a texture exported from the current KiCad copper and silkscreen layers. The fabric shell and lithium cells come from the mask assembly scene; the cells are shown parallel to the board in the exploded shot.

The HR closeup keeps D20–D22 on their existing 0805 footprints. Its package geometry comes from the manufacturer's [APT2012 STEP model](https://www.kingbrightusa.com/kcpn3d.asp?PN=APT2012ZGC); [green](https://www.kingbrightusa.com/images/catalog/SPEC/APT2012ZGC.pdf) and [red](https://www.kingbrightusa.com/images/catalog/SPEC/APT2012SURCK.pdf) APT2012 parts are 2.0 × 1.25 × 0.75 mm with water-clear lenses. These are visual package references, not a claim that the final HR emitters have been selected. The larger `XL-6028UWC-FB` package belongs to the bottom light emitters, not the HR sensor. The AFE4404 appears on its existing board location.

The tiny 0402 passives are left unmarked in the render, consistent with common package options. The ESP32 package marking is included in its closeup.
